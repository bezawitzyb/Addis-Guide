Communication, payments & transportation

Land. Connect. Ride.

Four steps to get set up when you arrive. Do them in this order, because telebirr is linked to your Ethiopian SIM.

  1. 1At the airport

    Get an Ethio telecom SIM card

    Buy an Ethio telecom SIM card at the airport using your passport. You need it for telebirr in the next step.

    Ethio telecomBring your passport
  2. 2Pay

    Download telebirr

    Open an account. It is linked to your SIM, and you can pay almost everywhere with it.

    telebirrEthio telecom
  3. 3Top up

    Download Taptap Send

    Use it to transfer money from home to your telebirr account.

    Taptap SendTaptap Send, Inc.
  4. 4Get around

    Download Yango or Feres

    Book rides around the city. Yango Lite is a smaller version of the Yango app.

A major museum and memorial commemorating Ethiopia’s victory at the Battle of Adwa in 1896. A powerful visit for understanding Ethiopian history, independence, and national identity.

10

Entoto Park

Set in the highlands above Addis Ababa, Entoto Park offers forest scenery, fresh mountain air, walking areas, viewpoints, and outdoor activities. A good escape from the busy city.

Entoto is cooler than the city centre, so bring an extra layer.

Good to know

A few tips.

Useful words (Amharic)

  • SelamHello
  • AmeseginalehuThank you
  • IshiOkay
  • Getting aroundAllow extra time for journeys. Traffic can be busy, particularly around central Addis and during peak periods.
  • MapsUse the Google Maps links in this guide to navigate to each destination. Check the exact venue name before setting off, as some places have similarly named locations.
  • Opening timesConfirm opening hours and access arrangements before visiting museums, parks, and nightlife venues.
  • Cultural experiencesFor a memorable Ethiopian evening, consider combining a traditional restaurant with live music or dance.
  • Mountain visitsEntoto is cooler than the city centre, so bring an extra layer.
  • Wedding eventsFollow the wedding hosts’ instructions for arrival times and transport to the private event locations.
